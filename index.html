A student-driven institute.
A shared pursuit of discovery.

We are an autonomous research institute connecting deep-space exoplanetary atmospheres with ancient biology and genomic divergence. Our work brings verifiable datasets, peer-grade articles and open pipelines into a shared framework for discovery.

PROGRAMME ADMISSIONS • WHO SHOULD APPLY

Who can join us?

CRII was built by and for students, regardless of formal institutional affiliation. We welcome curious, committed individuals across disciplines who want to contribute to frontier open science.

Undergraduates

Early-stage researchers looking to contribute to real-world science beyond coursework, receive mentorship and publish peer-grade research.

Apply as Fellow

Postgraduates

Master's and doctoral candidates who want to lead research streams, publish open datasets and mentor promising undergraduates.

Apply as Fellow

Self-Directed Researchers

Talented, self-taught individuals outside traditional academia who have deep knowledge and an appetite to produce verifiable science.

Apply as Fellow

Research Fellows

Experienced researchers and domain experts who contribute time to mentor cohorts and supervise specific research streams.

Meet team
Applicant Note: All CRII Fellowship positions are remote, open to applicants worldwide, and conducted under open-science principles.
